Data & Methodology

Clear definitions. Traceable sources.

The figures on this site are designed to remain understandable as the portfolio grows.

Project source

Project, organisation, country, funding, signature and start-date records are derived from the European Commission's CORDIS project records.

Visit CORDIS ↗

National Contact Points

The contact directory is maintained separately because contact responsibilities can change independently from project data.

Organisation network in the Story

Organisation positions are derived from the reported head-office city and matched to GeoNames coordinates. Records without a city use a country reference point. Country boundaries come from Natural Earth. Lines connect New Zealand and partner organisations appearing in the same project; their colours identify the Horizon Europe cluster.

Measurement rules

  • Distinct projects: each grant agreement is counted once.
  • Project–Country connections: each Project–Country pair is counted once, even when several organisations from that country participate.
  • Project value: the maximum EU contribution to the selected distinct projects.
  • Allocated to New Zealand: the sum of net EU contributions recorded for New Zealand participants.
  • Signed versus started: a signed project may have a future start date. The two concepts are reported separately where relevant.
  • Multiple filters: projects involving at least one selected country or cluster are included.
