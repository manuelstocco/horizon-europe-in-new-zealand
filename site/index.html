Horizon Europe in 60 seconds

One programme. Three pillars.
Many routes to discovery.

Start with the architecture. The visual moves from Horizon Europe's original allocation of resources to the collaborative pillar where New Zealand participates.

01

The EU's research and innovation programme

Ideas move through three connected pillars. Horizon Europe links frontier research, collaborative responses to global challenges and the journey from ideas to innovation.

02

Pillar II is built for collaboration

The largest pillar is collaborative by design. International consortia bring universities, research organisations, businesses and public bodies together around shared challenges.

03

New Zealand is associated to Pillar II

Association changes the terms of participation. New Zealand organisations can join and lead eligible consortia under conditions similar to organisations in EU Member States.

04

Six clusters organise the shared challenges

Six thematic gateways turn access into opportunity. Health, society, security, technology, climate and the bioeconomy provide routes into international collaboration.

Bubble area now reflects signed projects involving New Zealand, rather than programme resources.

The road to association

A relationship becomes a route into collaboration.

2009

A relationship already in motion

Cooperation did not begin in 2023. The EU–New Zealand Science and Technology Cooperation Agreement created a formal foundation for dialogue and shared research priorities.

14 yearsof formal cooperation before association

2022

The possibility becomes a process

The relationship gained a destination. Exploratory talks developed into formal negotiations. By December, the two sides had agreed the terms of New Zealand's association.

Explore → negotiate → agree

9 July 2023

New Zealand becomes associated

The door formally opened. The agreement is signed and provisionally applied. New Zealand becomes the first country beyond Europe's geographical neighbourhood to associate with Horizon Europe.

Pillar IIDirect access to collaborative research

26 October 2023

The first agreement in the portfolio

Access became activity in 109 days. The grant agreement for d@rts is signed, connecting New Zealand expertise to an international project on arts education, cultural literacy and social inclusion.

109 daysfrom association to the first recorded signature

1 January 2024

Collaboration begins on the ground

Four projects moved at once. d@rts, EU-CIEMBLY, VITAL and STELLA begin together — spanning culture, democracy, personalised health and plant resilience.

A network takes shape

One agreement becomes a growing portfolio.

The grant is only the beginning. Each project adds organisations, expertise and relationships that can carry into the next collaboration.

From projects to a network

Funding starts the work.
Connections extend its value.

The durable asset is the network. Every consortium adds expertise, facilities, data and relationships that reach beyond a single grant. For a geographically distant country, collaboration makes the world smaller.
