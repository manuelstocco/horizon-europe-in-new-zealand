How these figures are counted

Projects are distinct CORDIS grant records containing at least one organisation from the selected country. Organisations are deduplicated using the CORDIS organisation identifier where available. Country allocation sums recorded net EU contributions to the selected country’s organisations; zero or unreported allocations remain visible as such.

Active NZ–country links count a distinct New Zealand organisation–country organisation pair once per cluster when both participate in the same project. Organisation type, role and SME status are copied from the CORDIS XML record and are not inferred by this website.
